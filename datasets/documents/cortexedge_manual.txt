CortexEdge is a quality-constrained edge AI runtime. It routes queries between small local models, medium local models, local RAG, and cloud fallback. The INA219 sensor measures power consumption in milliwatts. The semantic cache threshold is 0.95 and the maximum CPU temperature is 75 degrees Celsius. The minimum free RAM is 1500 megabytes. Routing uses a weighted cost function combining latency, energy, memory, cloud spend and privacy risk.
